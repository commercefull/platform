> Shopify's app tax compounds. Commercefull ships loyalty, subscriptions, membership, reviews, returns, analytics, and compliance natively.

---

## The Problem: Shopify App Tax

Every feature a growing merchant needs — loyalty, subscriptions, reviews, returns, analytics — requires a separate paid app on Shopify. The sticker price looks small, but apps scale with orders, contacts, or revenue share. A $99 sticker becomes a four-figure line item as you grow.

> A full DTC app stack commonly runs **$300–$800/month near $1M revenue** and **$2,500–$6,000/month near $10M**.
> — Shopify App Pricing Benchmarks, 2026

## Methodology

Shopify app prices are published list prices from the Shopify App Store, verified August 2026. Subscription transaction fees (Recharge 1.49% + 19¢/order) are calculated on stated order volumes. Klaviyo pricing is based on published contact-tier rates. Gorgias pricing is based on published ticket-tier rates. Actual costs vary by store configuration, negotiated rates, and volume. See [Shopify App Pricing Benchmarks 2026](https://taylorsicard.com/blog/shopify-app-pricing-benchmarks-2026) for the full methodology.
